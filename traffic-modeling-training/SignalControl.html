Ch. 5 — pretimed signal timing (Webster) and unsignalized gap acceptance ? Pretimed signal (§5.2.2): two conflicting one-way movements, saturation flow s, lost time L per cycle. With flow ratios yi = qi/s, the split rule is Gi = (C−L)·yi/(y₁+y₂) (Eq. 5.15a) and Webster's cycle is C* = (1.5L + 5)/(1 − y₁ − y₂) (Eq. 5.15b) — about double the bare minimum Cmin = L/(1−y₁−y₂) (Eq. 5.16). Too short a cycle → constant overflows (stochastic delay explodes as x → 1); too long → everyone waits out long reds (deterministic delay grows ∝ C). The delay curve shows the tradeoff.

Gap acceptance (§5.1): a minor street enters only into main-street gaps: a gap h fits n = max{0, ⌊(h − Hc + M)/M⌋} vehicles (critical gap Hc, move-up time M — Table 5.1). Bunching the main street helps the minor street; a signal is the extreme version, giving combined capacity s(1 − L/C) (Eq. 5.2).

Scheduled Transportation Systems

Ch. 7 — passenger waiting time (§7.1) and schedule instability / bus bunching (§7.2.2.1) ? Waiting time: uninformed passengers arrive at rate λ; buses depart with headways {hk}. The cumulative diagram (Fig. 7.1) stacks one triangle per headway, giving total wait λΣh²/2 over λΣh passengers:
W = E[H]/2 · (1 + CV²(H))
Irregularity always hurts: same average headway, longer average wait — because more passengers arrive during the long headways. At CV = 1 (exponential headways) the wait doubles to E[H].

Bunching (Fig. 7.6): dwell time grows with the number boarding, which grows with the headway. A bus running late collects more passengers, dwells longer, falls later — while its follower speeds up. Headway errors grow exponentially along the route until buses pair. Holding control (a slack-based schedule) damps the instability at the price of longer scheduled trip times.

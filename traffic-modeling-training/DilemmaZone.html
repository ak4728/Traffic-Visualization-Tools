i For every approach speed, the blue curve is the closest distance from which a driver can still stop comfortably (x_stop), and the green curve is the farthest distance from which the vehicle can clear before red (x_clear). Where blue rises above green, drivers between the two can neither stop nor clear — the dilemma zone. Where green exceeds blue, both choices work — an option zone. Drag the pulsing dot to set speed and position; it syncs with the probe car on the road.

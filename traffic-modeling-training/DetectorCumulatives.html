Ch. 2 · Build cumulative curves from hourly detector counts (CDOT VOLUME exports)
Load detector files to build cumulative curves
iEach file is one detector-direction of hourly counts. Pick which station sits at the West end (E/W road) or North end (N/S road): for EB traffic the west station is upstream (arrivals) and the east one downstream (departures) — and vice versa for WB (for N/S: NB flows toward the north station). Because ramps between detectors add/remove traffic, the downstream curve is optionally rescaled so daily totals match; the upstream curve is shifted by free-flow travel time so any remaining horizontal gap is delay, the vertical gap is excess accumulation, and the shaded area is total delay. Hourly steps are smoothed with a monotone cubic so the curves look like fluid N-curves — toggle it off to see the raw staircase.
